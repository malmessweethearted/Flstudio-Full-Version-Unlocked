## Main Features
<a id="main-features"></a>

### Core Functionality
- **Step-based audio sequencer:** Record multiple tracks including instruments and voices, edit audio with tools like pitch correction and time-stretching.
- **MIDI Integration:** Utilize external MIDI controllers to input music notes directly into the piano roll for precise editing.
- **Live Performance Capabilities:** Carry out live performances with video effects, making it suitable for DJs and live musicians.
- **Extensive Effects Library:** Access a large number of effects such as reverb, delay, and filters to enhance your sound.
- **VST Support:** Includes support for VST, DX, and FL Native plugins, allowing you to expand your toolkit with additional instruments and effects.

### Advanced Capabilities
- **Modular Design:** Users can customize their workspace by utilizing only the components and functions they need, streamlining their workflow.
- **Audio Export Options:** Export your creations in various formats including WAV, MP3, OGG, or MIDI, making it easy to share your music.
- **Multi-Window Interface:** Navigate through five different windows (step sequencer, piano roll, playlist, mixer, and sample browser) for a comprehensive editing experience.
- **Plugin Support:** In addition to built-in plugins, users can purchase additional plugins to further expand their creative possibilities, compatible with DirectX and VST standards.
